# The AMZN 240 miss, and whether a rule would have caught it

2026-11-09. Asked by Adam after AMZN ran from 240 to about 270 with the desk saying no. Book rows B-1358 (miss review), B-1357 and B-1359 (GOOGL the same morning). Script: `scripts/earnings-reclaim.mjs`. No rule was changed by this note.

## What happened

- **9/10:** B-310 wrote a 236-241 zone, limit 240, stop 225.50, targets 262 and 275. The same night B-319 stood it down because capital went to IREN and readiness was 49. Nothing reached the broker.
- **10/22:** the plan expired. The lowest low while it was alive was 244.30.
- **10/29:** the print. The desk read the guide as up (B-1172) and set a new zone of 226-231, with Adam's yes for Friday (B-1176).
- **10/30:** open 232.53. The first 5-minute bar low of 229.36 traded inside the zone, which corrects B-1266 and B-1248 ("never traded"). The first 30-minute bar closed 239.195, over the 231 ceiling: NO TRADE (B-1182). AMZN settled 240.35. No plan on the book could fill there.
- **11/02 to 11/03:** no trade at 250.25, 250.14, 253.53, 247.04 and 250.07.
- **11/04:** gap to 264.82. The 265.34 pivot traded. B-1266 wrote the lesson: after a raised guide, a pullback-only zone is a plan to miss the move.
- **11/09:** B-1351 wrote another pullback-only zone, the same shape.

An entry at the 239.195 bar close with a stop at 229.00 (1.32 ATR) and a target of 265.34 paid 2.56 to 1, and the target printed three sessions later. The market leg of the Capital Competition Test would still have refused it: AMZN trailed SPY on both windows.

## The bought-back gap-down test

Fixed before looking: earnings reaction day, open gaps down 3%+, close 2%+ above the open and in the upper half of the range, trailing SPY on both the 20- and 60-session windows. Buy the close, stop the lower of just under that day's low and 1.0 ATR, target 2R, stop checked first, out at session 20. Daily bars stand in for the first 30-minute bar. 426 names, 13,119 earnings days, 2017 to 2026. AMZN 10/30 is not in the sample (the filing cache ends 10/11) and qualifies by hand.

The rule beats every comparison and misses the 3.0 bar. Split after the first look, so not a finding: mega caps 14 cases, 42.9% wins, +0.10R; large caps 77 cases, 48.1%, +0.22R; mid caps 78 cases, 57.7%, +0.31R. AMZN's own instances: 2021-10-29 target, 2022-10-28 stop, 2026-02-06 stop.

## Reading

The miss was real and the desk had the company right. No rule tested here shows an edge that would have made 240 a yes in advance. The Capital Competition no's mostly held up. The gap in the system is one of kind: the desk is a swing-trade judge, and owning and adding to a company over months is a different job with different numbers.
