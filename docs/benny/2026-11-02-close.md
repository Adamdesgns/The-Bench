The lesson today is the difference between a rotation and a selloff. QQQ, the Nasdaq-100 fund, fell 1.4 percent to 681.13 and the chip fund SMH fell 2.8. In the same session the consumer fund XLY rose 2.4, the bank fund XLF 1.8, and the Dow fund DIA 0.4. A selloff is money leaving stocks for cash. A rotation is money leaving one group of stocks for another. Today was a rotation: the money left AI hardware and bought the rest of the market. VIX, the fear index, closed 21.15.
