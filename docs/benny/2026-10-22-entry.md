A quick reminder of the rule. Each plan sets a buy zone, which is a narrow price range. The first 30-minute bar of the day (8:30 to 9:00 CT) has to close inside that range before anything gets bought. If it closes above the range, the stock has already left. The stop-loss (the price where you sell to cap the loss) stays where it was, so buying higher means risking more for the same reward. Below 2-to-1 (two dollars of possible reward for every dollar at risk), we pass.
