That gap is the lesson. When one corner of the market falls hard and the rest closes flat or up, money is leaving one trade, not leaving stocks. That is called rotation. A real flight from risk shows up everywhere at once, and junk bonds fall with it. Today they did not. Before you read the index, check breadth: how many parts of the market actually went down.
