The rule for anyone new. Each plan sets a buy zone, a narrow price range where the math still works. The first 30-minute bar of the day (8:30 to 9:00 CT) has to close inside that range before anything gets bought. Close above it and the stock has already left. The stop-loss (the price where you sell to cap the loss) stays where it was, so buying higher means risking more dollars for the same reward. Under 2-to-1 (two dollars of possible reward for every dollar at risk), I pass. Chasing is how a right idea turns into a wrong trade.
