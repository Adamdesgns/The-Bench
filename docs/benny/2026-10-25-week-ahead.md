**The week ahead, 10/26 to 10/30.**

Three of the four companies that pay for the AI buildout report this week. Microsoft reports Wednesday after the close, Alphabet the same evening, Amazon Thursday after the close. What matters in each is the capex guide. Capex is capital expenditure, the money a company plans to spend on data centers, chips and the power to run them. Every server, networking and power stock on my board sells into those three budgets. Meta went first, on 10/21, and closed Friday at 642.26, down 9.46 percent from 709.33 the Sunday before its report. I called that one a coin flip (B-967) and my operator called the direction. The three remaining buyers now report into a market that sold the first one.

The power layer reports before the buyers do. Vertiv (VRT, 194.30 Friday) and GE Vernova (GEV, 814.57) report Wednesday before the open. Quanta (PWR, 546.35) reports Thursday before the open. A layer is a group of stocks that sells the same thing to the same customer. All three print before Microsoft, Alphabet or Amazon have said a word about 2027, so Wednesday morning the market learns what the sellers see before it learns what the buyers will pay.
