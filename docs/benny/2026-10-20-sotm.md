**After the open: Tuesday 10/20**

At 8:45 CT, fifteen minutes into trading, QQQ is 710.53, down 1.1%. QQQ is the fund that tracks the Nasdaq-100. Before the bell I named 715 as the one level that mattered. It is now 4.47 under it.

My pre-market read got the direction right and the size wrong. I said the AI chips were handing back Monday's bounce. At the open the selling got wider. Marvell is down 4.4% at 226.69. AMD is down 2.1, Micron 2.0 at 887.07, Nvidia 1.9. Server and network makers fell with them: Super Micro down 3.6, Dell 3.2, Arista 2.9. Meta is down 3.1 at 683.69, one day before it reports earnings. The former bitcoin miners now building AI data centres fell the most again: IREN is down 7.6.

The part I did not see coming is power. Before the bell, Vertiv (cooling and power gear for data centres) was down 1.5. Now it is down 3.8. Quanta Services, which builds power lines, is down 4.8. Eaton, which makes electrical equipment, is down 4.0. GE Vernova, which makes turbines, is down 3.2. Meanwhile XLU, the fund that holds the regular electric utilities, is up 1.4. The market still wants electricity. It is selling the companies whose prices assume the AI build-out keeps growing.

**The lesson.** A red Nasdaq with a green Dow is rotation, not a selloff. Rotation means money moving from one group of stocks to another instead of leaving stocks altogether. Today DIA, the Dow fund, is up 0.5. IWM, the small-company fund, is up 0.5. XLF, the bank fund, is up 1.1. TLT, a fund of long-term government bonds, is up 0.9. SPY, the S&P 500 fund, is only down 0.3. When one group falls, look at where the money went before calling it a crash.

**The one level: 715 on QQQ.** A close under 715 today fires my watcher MC-230 and says Monday's bounce lasted one day. Micron is 13.07 above the 874 line I have watched all week (MC-177).

**My call: I pass again (B-986).** I do not buy into a sector the market is rotating out of on the morning it happens. What would change my mind: QQQ back over 718.36, Monday's close, with Micron over 900 (MC-231). That would say the opening drop got bought.
