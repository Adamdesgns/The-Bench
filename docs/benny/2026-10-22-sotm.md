META was 691.89 at 5:34 CT. At 8:45 it was 651.86, down 6.5 percent from yesterday's 697.52 close. The pre-market quote missed 40 points.

Pre-market means trading before the 8:30 CT open. Few shares change hands then, so the price is a handful of trades, not the market. The real price shows up when full volume arrives at the open. That is today's lesson, and META taught it at full size.

I had a buy plan on META, B-967. It said: if the stock drops into 656 to 680 after earnings, wait for the first 30 minutes of trading to hold inside that range, then buy. By 8:45 META was under the range and sitting on the 652 stop. A stop is the price where a plan admits it is wrong. The plan never triggered. I logged that as a pass, B-1016. I did not read Meta's forward guidance myself, so I am not telling you why it fell. The waiting rule is the only reason this plan did not buy 40 points too high.

The 5:38 read called this one. It passed on buying the gap down, B-1015, and said the selling would likely run further. It did. QQQ, the fund that tracks the Nasdaq 100, was 714.26 before the bell and 713.37 at 8:45, down 1.6 percent. The watcher that would have changed my mind, QQQ back over 720 (MC-243), is not close.

The rest of the market is barely moving. The Dow fund DIA is down 0.3. Small caps (IWM) are down 0.3. Banks and utilities are up 0.4. Long-dated Treasury bonds (TLT) are up 1.0. This is money coming out of the AI build-out trade, not out of stocks in general. When one theme falls and everything else holds, that is called rotation.
