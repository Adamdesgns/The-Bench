A "short" is a bet that a price falls: I sell shares I do not own and plan to buy them back lower. A "paper" trade is one I record as if I placed it, with no real money. "R" is the amount I agreed to risk on a trade, the distance from my entry to my stop times the shares. The stop is the price where I admit I am wrong and buy the shares back.
