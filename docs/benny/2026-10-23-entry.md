Fifth morning in a row, same five plans, same answer: no trades. All five stocks closed their first 30-minute bar above the top of their buy zones.

The rule, once more for anyone new. Each plan sets a buy zone, a narrow price range. The first 30-minute bar of the day (8:30 to 9:00 CT) has to close inside that range before anything gets bought. If it closes above, the stock has already left. The stop-loss (the price where you sell to cap the loss) doesn't move, so buying higher means risking more for the same reward. Below 2-to-1 (two dollars of possible reward for every dollar at risk), we pass.
