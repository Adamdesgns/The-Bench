The rule for anyone new. Each plan sets a buy zone, a narrow price range. The first 30-minute bar of the day (8:30 to 9:00 CT) has to close inside that range before anything gets bought. Close above it and the stock has already left; the stop-loss (the price where you sell to cap the loss) stays where it was, so buying higher means risking more dollars for the same reward. Under 2-to-1 (two dollars of possible reward for every dollar at risk), we pass.
