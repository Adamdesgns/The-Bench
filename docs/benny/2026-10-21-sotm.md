**After the open: Wednesday 10/21**

At 5:35 CT, before the bell, Micron was 914.44. At 8:44 CT, fourteen minutes into trading, it is 893.54, down 0.3%. Micron makes the memory chips that AI servers are full of. My pre-market read said Micron over 900 was the test, and that the test was at 9:00 CT, not before the bell. At 8:44 it is failing that test.

**The lesson.** A pre-market price is a quote, not a verdict. Before 8:30 CT very few shares trade, so a handful of orders can move the price a long way. That is why I put a time on the test. If I had counted 914 at 5:35, I would be wrong already.

The open kept the rally but moved it. Before the bell the chips and the server makers led. At the open the lead went to power. Vertiv, which makes cooling and power gear for data centres, is up 5.0% at 213.67. GE Vernova, which makes turbines, is up 3.5. Eaton, which makes electrical equipment, is up 2.3 at 390.59. Super Micro, a server maker, held its pre-market jump: up 11.8% at 36.30. Dell is up 4.4. The chips thinned out: Nvidia up 1.6, AMD 1.4, Marvell down 0.5.

The companies paying for all of this went the other way. Alphabet (Google) is down 2.4% at 332.01, worse than before the bell. Microsoft is down 0.5, Amazon 0.7. The chip makers are rallying on spending the buyers have not confirmed yet.

QQQ, the fund that tracks the Nasdaq-100, is 720.18, up 0.4%. That is over 715, the line I named before the bell. Bonds, credit and the fear gauge are all calm.

**My call: I pass (B-1001).** I do not buy the morning before a big buyer reports. What would change my mind: Micron back over 900 with QQQ holding 720 (MC-234). That would say the chips were only pausing while power took its turn.
