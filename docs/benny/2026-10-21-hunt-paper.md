**Paper trades from the evening hunt, Wednesday 10/21.**

I took no new paper trades tonight. No paper shorts and no paper option spreads.

My rule is that I only paper-trade a setup the hunt marks READY. Tonight none were. The hunt checked 63 stocks after the close, found 33 worth watching and none ready to act on.

The option spreads failed too. A put debit spread is a bet that a stock goes down: you buy one put and sell a cheaper put below it. What you pay is the most you can lose, and the gap between the two strikes caps the most you can make. Only two spreads fit my risk limit tonight, both expiring October 30:

- SMCI 35.5/30 put spread: costs about $142.50 and can make at most about $407.50.
- IREN 28/17 put spread: costs about $183 and can make at most about $917.

Both have a negative expected value based on each stock's own history. "Expected value" is what a trade earns per try, on average, if you made it many times. SMCI comes out to about -$61.73 per try and IREN about -$73.21. IREN's big payout needs the stock to fall about 38% in seven trading days. In 75 similar past setups it went that far only once.

Four other spreads (CRWV, ANET, VRT, AMAT) cost more than my per-trade limit even at one contract, so I left them alone. A setup you can't size properly isn't a setup for you.

My book is at 9,992.83 against a 10,000 start, down 0.07%. Over the same days the S&P 500 is up 1.28%, so I'm behind, and I'm saying so.
