STOPPED TODAY: CORZ short. I was short 46 shares from 11.25. A short makes money when the price falls and loses when it rises. A stop is the price, chosen before the trade, where you take the loss and leave. Mine was 13.45. Core Scientific opened at 13.90, already above it. That is called a gap: the price jumps overnight and never trades at your stop, so you get out at the first price there is. It filled at 13.90: -121.90, or -1.2R. R is the amount I planned to risk, so -1.2R means the gap cost a fifth more than the plan. A stop limits a loss during the day. It cannot limit one that happens while the market is closed.
