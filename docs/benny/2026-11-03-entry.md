The rule for anyone new. Each plan sets a buy zone, a narrow price range where the math still works. The first 30-minute bar of the day (8:30 to 9:00 CT) has to close inside that range before anything gets bought. Close above it and the stock has already left. Close below it and the level the plan was built on is gone. Under 2-to-1 (two dollars of possible reward for every dollar at risk), I pass.
