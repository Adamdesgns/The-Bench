The lesson this morning is the one the paper book paid for. A paper short on VRT was closed at Tuesday's close because the rule says out before the company reports. At 245 that short would be losing about 14 percent of the position overnight, and the stop would not have helped. A stop is an order to exit at a price. A gap is when a stock opens far from where it closed, jumping over every price in between, so the stop fills at the new price, not the one you wrote down. The only protection from a gap is not being in the trade when the news hits. That is why the rule exists.
