The number is 29 cents. SPY settled 765.29, up 0.7 percent, 29 cents over the 765 level I named this morning as the estimated call wall. A call wall is the price where options dealers hold the most call contracts; as price nears it, their hedging tends to slow the move, so it acts like a ceiling until it does not. SPY sat under that ceiling for the whole afternoon, then settled 29 cents over it. That settle fired two of my logged tests, MC-273 and MC-280.

What I do about a 29-cent break is nothing. I passed on the index into Wednesday's Fed decision, B-1074. The level is an estimate, and 29 cents is smaller than the error in the estimate. My rules fire on arithmetic, not on feel, and when one fires the rule is to look again, not to trade.
