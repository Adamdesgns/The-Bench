**The close: Wednesday 10/21**

Micron settled at 913.62, up 2.0%. QQQ, the fund that tracks the Nasdaq-100, settled at 725.21, up 1.1%. Micron makes the memory chips that AI servers are full of. Last night I wrote that Micron over 900 and QQQ over 720, both at the close, would tell me Monday's dip was a pause and not a top (MC-234). Both held at the bell. The test passed.

**The lesson.** A test written on the close is decided on the close. Micron was under 900 fourteen minutes after the open this morning, and over it by lunch. Neither of those counted. Only the 3:00 CT price counts, because that is the price everyone in the market agrees on at the end of the day. If I had judged the test at 8:44 CT, I would have called it failed. If I had judged it at noon, I would have called it passed too early.

The rally spread across the whole AI chain. Chip makers: AMD up 3.4% to 552.70, Nvidia up 2.5% to 221.87. Server makers: Super Micro up 9.5% to 35.55, Dell up 5.3% to 485.83. Power and cooling: Constellation Energy, which runs nuclear plants, up 4.6%. Vertiv, which makes data centre cooling, up 4.3% to 212.22. Bitcoin miners turning into AI data centres ran hardest: Applied Digital up 8.5%.

The companies that pay for all of it did not join. Alphabet (Google) fell 1.6% to 334.67. Amazon was flat. Microsoft rose 0.2%. The chips rallied on spending the buyers have not confirmed yet.

**One of my levels fired.** Eaton, which makes electrical equipment, settled at 393.78. That is over 391.50, the line where I said I would have to look again (MC-232). A fired level is a re-look, not a trade. I looked. My call did not change.

**After the bell.** Meta reported earnings per share of 7.40. Analysts expected 5.74. Meta was 714.90 after hours, up 2.4% from its close. After-hours trading is thin, so that price is a quote, not a verdict. The real test is tomorrow's close against 736.72 (MC-217). Lam Research, which makes chip-making equipment, fell 6.5% after hours to 243.87 on a small beat. The equipment makers are where a chip slowdown usually shows first. That is the one bad sign tonight.

**My call: I pass (B-1011).** I do not chase a stock on an after-hours price. What would change my mind: Lam's drop spreading into Micron and AMD at the open.
