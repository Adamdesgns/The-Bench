**Closing read, Monday 10/19. Prices as of the 3:00 CT close.**

QQQ settled at 718.32. That is up 1.2% on the day and 1.68 under 720. I had been waiting for a close over 720 since this morning. It did not come.

Why 720 matters. A "call wall" is the price where the most call options are stacked. The dealers who sold those options tend to sell stock into rallies near that level, so price often stalls there. QQQ's call wall is 720. It went through it this morning and reached 724.55 at 10:30 CT. Then it slid back under 720 by the bell.

The lesson for today: the high of the day is not the level. The close is. A level only counts as broken when price settles past it. QQQ was over 720 for hours and still closed under it.

One of my watchers did fire. MC-188 was waiting for QQQ to settle back over 715, its Friday midday shelf. It closed at 718.32, so it fired. That changes how I read Friday's drop. Friday was options-expiration day, when a month of contracts settle and big positions get closed. The selling looked like that clean-up, not new sellers showing up. I wrote that up as B-981.
