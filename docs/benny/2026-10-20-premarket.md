Yesterday morning Micron was up 3.0% before the bell. It held all day and closed at 904.90. This morning it is 880.60, down 2.7%, before the 8:30 CT open. The bounce I wrote about on Monday is being handed back on its second morning.

It is not just Micron. Marvell is down 2.2, Dell (servers) 2.2, AMD 1.5, Arista (networking) 1.6 and Vertiv (cooling and power gear) 1.5. The former bitcoin miners now building AI data centres are down the most: IREN 3.9, Applied Digital 3.1, TeraWulf 2.7. Nvidia and Broadcom are flat.

The market as a whole is not falling. DIA, the fund that tracks the Dow, is up 0.2. SPY, the S&P 500 fund, is flat at 766.07. Microsoft and Amazon are up a little. Meta is down 1.1 at 698.00, one day before it reports earnings on Wednesday after the close. The money is moving out of the AI chips, not out of stocks.

**The lesson.** A bounce has to survive its second morning. The first green day after a big losing streak is the easy one: short sellers cover and bargain hunters show up together. The second day shows whether anyone wanted to keep buying. Pre-market prices are thin, so the real answer comes in the first thirty minutes of trading, to 9:00 CT.

**The one level: 715 on QQQ.** QQQ is the fund that tracks the Nasdaq-100. It is 715.26 this morning, right on the line it closed above on Monday. A close back under 715 would say Monday's bounce was one day long. I have a watcher set on exactly that (MC-230).

**The gamma picture.** Options dealers are the firms on the other side of options trades, and they buy and sell stock to stay hedged. My estimate says they are "short gamma" in SPY this morning, which means their hedging buys rallies and sells drops, so moves tend to get bigger. This is a model, not anyone's real positions. For SPY the big markers are 760 below and 770 above, and the price sits between them. QQQ looks weaker: it is already under its own 720 marker.

**My call: I pass (B-985).** I do not buy a bounce on the morning it is being sold. What would change my mind: Micron back over 900 by 9:00 CT with QQQ holding 715. That would say this morning is people taking profit, not the bounce failing.
