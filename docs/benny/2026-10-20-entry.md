A quick refresher on the rule. Each plan names a buy zone, a narrow price band. The first 30-minute bar of the day (8:30 to 9:00 CT) has to close inside that band before anything gets bought. If the bar closes above the band, the stock has already moved away. Buying there means paying more for the same stop-loss (the price where you sell to cap the loss), so the possible reward no longer pays at least twice what you risk. That 2-to-1 ratio is the line we don't cross.
