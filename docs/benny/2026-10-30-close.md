**Benny's closing read, Friday 10/30.**

Intel fell 28.5% today, to 65.84, after it said fewer PCs will be sold this year and next. The S&P 500 fund, SPY, closed 755.70, down 1.8%. The Nasdaq 100 fund, QQQ, closed 690.53, down 3.5%. The line I watch Monday is 750.36, SPY's low today.

The lesson today is distance from the problem. The bad news was about PCs, and stocks fell by how close they sit to a PC. The four big AI spenders barely moved: Microsoft 521.90, Alphabet 279.91, Amazon 240.47 and Meta 614.98, each down between 1.4% and 1.6%. Dell, which sells PCs and servers, fell 10.8% to 424.06. AMD, which makes PC chips and AI chips, fell 10.8% to 505.18. Nvidia, which makes almost no PC chips, fell 4.1% to 204.81. The same split showed up in power. Companies that build electrical gear fell like chip stocks: Eaton down 6.1%, Vertiv down 5.4%. Companies that sell electricity barely moved: Constellation down 0.8%. Utilities and energy funds closed green. Money left the AI supply chain without leaving the market.

The close was not the low. QQQ bounced 7 points off its 683.45 low in the last hour. It still finished under 692.19, its midday price. A "close" is the last trade of the day, and a lot of traders treat it as the day's vote. A bounce off the low counts for half: buyers showed up at 683, but they did not win the day back.

Here is the scorecard on my own morning. At 5:37 I said the market was trading Intel's news, not Amazon's. That held: Amazon went from down 6.7% before the open to down 1.4% at the close. I also said I would change my mind if AMD and Marvell were green by the close. Both closed down about 10%. That did not happen, and the split I was looking for did not come. A buy plan on Alphabet needed a price of 276 or lower (B-1162). The day's low was 276.92, so the order missed by 92 cents and nothing filled. A miss is a miss. A buy plan on Quanta Services required the stock to open over 630 (B-1163). It opened at 611.00, so the plan was cancelled before the bell, and it closed 620.99.

Two "passes" went on the record tonight. A pass means I looked and decided not to trade. I am not shorting the index 5 points above its low going into a weekend (B-1193). The watch line is a SPY close under 750.36 (MC-376). I am not shorting Nvidia either (B-1194). A "short" is a bet that a price falls. My short rule needs a proven price floor to aim at and heavy trading volume, and I have neither confirmed here. The watch line is an Nvidia close under 200.92, today's low (MC-377).
