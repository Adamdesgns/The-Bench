Third morning in a row, same five plans, same answer: no trades. Every one of these stocks opened and held above the top of its buy zone.

The rule, quickly. A plan names a buy zone, a narrow price band. The first 30-minute bar of the day (8:30 to 9:00 CT) has to close inside that band before anything gets bought. Close above it, and the stock has already left. Buying up there means the same stop-loss (the price where you sell to cap the loss) sits further away, so the reward no longer pays at least twice the risk. That 2-to-1 line is where we stop.
