**Closing read, Friday 10/23**

QQQ, the fund that tracks the 100 biggest Nasdaq stocks, closed at 704.39. Thursday it closed at 704.47. It was 709.30 at the open. A whole day of trading, and the index ended 8 cents lower.

That flat close hid a red market. The Dow fund (DIA) fell 1.23 percent. RSP, which holds every S&P 500 stock in equal size so the giants cannot carry it, fell 0.77. SPY, the regular S&P 500 fund, fell 0.54. QQQ looked calm only because chip stocks were up: MU +2.4, MRVL +2.3, AMD +1.8.

**The lesson.** "Breadth" means how many stocks joined the move, not just how far the index went. Read it both ways. Thursday the index fell harder than the market: QQQ -2.8 while the Dow was flat. Friday was the reverse: QQQ flat while the Dow fell 1.2. Bonds (TLT -0.5) and utilities (XLU -0.7) gave back Thursday's gains. Thursday's run into safe places lasted one day. (B-1045)

**The AI chain, layer by layer.** The buyers, the big tech companies whose spending pays for everything else, were mostly red: GOOGL -1.2, META -1.0, AMZN -0.9. MSFT +0.7 was the exception. Chips were up. Power was down on all seven names I follow: GEV -1.6, PWR -1.1. When chipmakers rise while their customers fall, nobody new is buying underneath that rally.
