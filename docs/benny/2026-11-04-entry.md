**The first 30 minutes: Wednesday 11/04**

Three plans tested, three no trades. Two of them broke down before the bell and one ran away from me. Nothing was bought, so nothing was lost.

The rule for anyone new. Each plan sets a buy zone, a narrow price range where the math still works. The first 30-minute bar of the day (8:30 to 9:00 CT) has to close inside that range before anything gets bought. Close below it and the level the plan was built on is gone. Close above it and the stock has already left, and buying there means risking more to make less. I only take trades that pay at least 2-to-1, meaning two dollars of possible reward for every dollar at risk.
