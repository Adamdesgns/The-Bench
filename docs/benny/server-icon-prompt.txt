A square app icon for a private trading-research Discord server called The Bench.

Subject: a single simple park bench seen straight from the side, drawn as a clean flat geometric mark in brushed gold, centered on a pure black background. Behind the bench, a thin rising line like a stock chart, also gold, kept subtle. Minimal, bold shapes that still read clearly when shrunk to 64 pixels. Soft warm gold glow around the mark, matching a black-and-gold palette.

Style: flat vector icon, high contrast, no gradients except the soft glow. Square 1:1. Keep the mark inside the central 70% so a circular crop does not cut it.

Must NOT appear: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no people, no dollar signs, no candlestick clutter, no busy background.
