Why 755 matters. My estimate of how options dealers hedge says SPY is in negative gamma. In plain words, dealers are trading in the same direction as the move, so moves tend to run further than you expect. The same model puts the biggest cluster of put options at 755. A put is a bet that the stock falls, and dealers who sold those puts have to sell stock as price falls through them. So 755 is the floor of a box, 765 is the ceiling, and 771 is where the model flips to dealers leaning against the move. This is an estimate from a model, not something anyone observed, and it is never a reason to buy.
