# PRE-MARKET read - 2026-10-30 05:37 CT (data pulled 05:35 CT)

The number: Amazon spent 77.3B on capex last quarter, about 18B more than expected, and AMD is down 8.1% pre-market anyway. The biggest buyer of AI hardware wrote a larger check and the chips it buys are falling. The thing to watch is which piece of bad news the tape is actually trading.

It is trading Intel, not Amazon. Intel guided Q4 well under estimates and its CEO put PC shipments down 12% this year and another 10% in 2027 (wire reports, not a filing I read). PC names took it straight: DELL -9.6%, SMCI -5.3%. AI silicon is being sold alongside them: AMD -8.1%, MRVL -5.8%, MU -4.8%, AVGO -3.6%, NVDA -2.7%. Power fell too: VRT -6.6%, ETN -3.2%. The miners turned AI landlords are off 5-6%. Buyers held up best apart from Amazon itself: MSFT -0.7%, GOOGL -0.6%, META -1.5%, AMZN -6.7% on an EPS miss (1.89 vs about 1.96) and negative 42.4B trailing free cash flow. AWS grew 39%.

The lesson here is the layer map. A PC forecast is not a statement about hyperscaler GPU demand. Amazon's spend says demand for AI chips is still going up. Intel's warning says the PC market is shrinking. The tape is pricing both stories as one today. That gap closes one of two ways: AI silicon recovers while PC names stay down, or the selling is really about everyone's capex being paid for with negative free cash flow. In that case the buyers crack next.

Gamma is how much options dealers have to buy or sell to stay hedged as the price moves. SPY is in negative gamma (estimated), so dealers trade with the move and moves tend to extend. Pre-market SPY 761.93 is below the king node and put wall at 765, under the 770 call wall, and about 9 points under the 771.12 flip. Until SPY gets back above 765, a slide has nothing in the options book to slow it.
