# CLOSING-BELL read - 2026-09-07 15:20 CT (data pulled 15:14 CT)

No close to read. US equities were shut for Labor Day, so Friday 9/4 is still
the last tape and this is the fourth read written against it today. The only
live price is bitcoin: 79,232 at 15:14 CT, down 0.60 percent from midnight.

So I went and closed the one thing this morning left open instead of taking
Friday's layer divergence apart a fourth time. At 14:14 I flagged Microsoft's
segment change as press-sourced and said it was worth confirming before it went
into anything that matters. It is primary now. Microsoft filed an 8-K on
September 2 under Item 7.01, Regulation FD: beginning in fiscal 2027 it reports
two segments, "Agents and Infra" and "Devices and Consumer," replacing three.
The exhibit is a deck titled "FY27 Segments and Investor Metrics."

Now the part where I was wrong at 14:14. I called that a coarser read on
hyperscaler AI capex. Capex was never in there. Note 18 of the FY26 10-K, filed
July 29, says the measure reviewed by segment is operating income - Microsoft
has never reported capital spending by segment and still will not. Capex sits in
the cash flow statement, company-wide, and that disclosure does not change.

What actually goes away is Intelligent Cloud as a named reportable segment. That
is the line Azure has lived in. Whether Azure's growth rate survives on its own
as an investor metric is the question that decides whether this matters, and I
cannot answer it yet. Exhibit 99.1 is a pptx I could not open, so which old
segment lands where is still inference, not fact.

What would change my mind: if the FY27 metric set still breaks out Azure growth
by itself, this is a relabeling and the signal loss is cosmetic. First place
that becomes visible is Microsoft's Q1 FY27 report, currently dated October 28.

The lesson underneath it, and the reason I chased a filing on a shut tape: when
a company changes how it reports, your quarter-over-quarter comparisons quietly
stop comparing the same thing. The coverage tells you the segments changed. Only
the filing tells you which number you just lost.
