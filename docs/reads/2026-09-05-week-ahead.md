## IRAN — where this actually stands

This is a live shooting war, not a headline risk. The facts, dated:

- **9/1** — US CENTCOM began striking IRGC targets inside Iran, after two oil
  tankers (one Saudi, one South Korean) were hit by projectiles in the Strait of
  Hormuz. WTI jumped 4.7% to $90.46, Brent to $95.05. The S&P fell 0.7% the same
  day; XLE was the strongest sector ETF, +1.1%.
- **9/2** — Reuters called it the worst US-Iran exchange of fire since July.
  Israeli strikes on Hezbollah in Lebanon and Hamas in Gaza, Houthi attacks in
  the Red Sea. Trump says there is **no timeline** for ending it, and a US naval
  blockade of Iranian ports is in place.
- **9/3** — Treasury launched **"Operation Economic Outcast,"** a maximum-pressure
  campaign to defund the IRGC. Pompeo is publicly pushing to cut China's
  purchases of Iranian crude (~500,000 bbl/day, paid in yuan) **before Xi Jinping
  arrives at the White House on Sept 24**.
- **The single most important fact: HORMUZ IS STILL OPEN.** Energy Secretary
  Chris Wright says more than 17 million barrels transited on Monday. The entire
  oil premium is priced on a disruption that has not happened yet.

Where that leaves the tape: **WTI Oct settled $91.48 Friday, Brent Nov $95.80.**
Crude is **+57% from the 12/31 close of $57.42**. The energy sector is **+44.8%
YTD against roughly 13% for the S&P**. Pump gas averages $4.14, diesel $5.78.
Separately, Ukrainian drone strikes have pushed Russian crude output to a 17-year
low of 9.88M bpd — the supply squeeze is two-sided and neither side is American
policy.

And the politics are turning. Trump's approval is down across key metrics, with

## DJT — you asked me to back it with Iran. I can't, and here is the arithmetic

**The mechanism you're pointing at is real, and it's genuinely clever.** In
August, Trump Media launched the **Truth API**: $60,000–$100,000 a month for
real-time, low-latency access to Trump's Truth Social posts. On the Q2 call the
company said it had signed **10-plus paying customers**. Trump announces Iran
policy on Truth Social *first* — the 8/11 compensation demand to Tehran was a
Truth post that moved the tape before any wire carried it. Elizabeth Warren's
actual complaint is that he is selling Wall Street early access to economic and
**national security** information. So yes: DJT literally monetizes Iran headline
latency. That is a coherent thesis and I understand why you flagged it.

**It dies on two numbers.**

**SIZE.** Ten customers at the $100K top of the range is about **$12 million a
year**. DJT's market cap is **$2.504 billion**. That is **0.5%**. Q2 revenue for
the entire company was **$1.7 million**. Meanwhile the Bitcoin stash is **$900
million — 36% of the whole market cap**. DJT is a bitcoin-holding sentiment
vehicle with 31 employees and a -1.91 PE. The API is a rounding error on the
thing that prices it. And that one Iran-linked revenue line is currently being
**sued by media groups seeking to shut it down**.

**SIGN.** DJT trades on Trump political capital. Iran is *subtracting* from it —
approval falling, MAGA support for the war halved, 18 dead, $37.5B spent, $4.14
gas. More Iran is not more DJT. It is less.

**And the falsification test already ran, this week.** 9/1 through 9/4 was the
most Iran-headline-dense stretch of the quarter — CENTCOM striking IRGC targets,
tankers hit in Hormuz, a new Treasury pressure campaign. If the thesis were
right, that is the week DJT rips. Instead it closed **$9.01 Friday, down 5.4% on
the day**, in the bottom third of a 20-day range of **7.90–10.23**, ATR(14)
$0.53, volume 4.50M on a 4.17M average — ordinary participation, no accumulation.
The thesis got the exact week it needed and the tape voted against it.
