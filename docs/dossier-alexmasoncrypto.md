# DOSSIER — @AlexMasonCrypto "I called the exact top and bottom"

**Prepared:** 2026-08-19 · **Source of record:** X API v2 full-archive search (`/2/tweets/search/all`), authenticated app-only
**Purpose:** verify three specific public claims against the timestamped archive
**Verdict:** **Both headline claims are false.** One is impossible; the other is contradicted by his own posts on the exact day.

---

## 1. THE CLAIM UNDER TEST

Posted 2026-08-19, at the end of a thread headlined "BITCOIN IS BEING MANIPULATED, AND I HAVE PROOF":

> "For the record, I was the only one publicly calling the exact bottom at $16,000 three years ago and the top at $126,000 in October. If you missed those calls, don't worry. I'll call the next one too. Turn notifications on."

Source post: https://x.com/AlexMasonCrypto/status/2090133773081854263

Three separable assertions:
- **A.** He publicly called the ~$16,000 Bitcoin bottom (Nov 2022).
- **B.** He publicly called the $126,000 Bitcoin top (Oct 2025).
- **C.** He was **the only one** to do either.

---

## 2. METHOD (state this in the visual — it's what makes it credible)

- Queried X's **full-archive search**, which reaches back to 2006, restricted to `from:AlexMasonCrypto`.
- Cross-checked with the **counts endpoint** (`/2/tweets/counts/all`) for independent per-year post volumes.
- Every quoted post below carries its **real post ID and permalink** — all independently clickable and verifiable.
- Market prices verified separately (see §3).

**Known limits — include these, they strengthen the piece:**
1. Full-archive search **does not return deleted posts.** Absence proves he did not *publicly and durably* call it; it cannot prove he never typed it.
2. It does not cover Telegram, YouTube, or paid groups. His claim says "publicly," which is what was tested.
3. Two endpoints disagree slightly on 2022 volume (search: 1 post; counts: 0). Either way the conclusion is unchanged.

## 10. WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

Say this plainly in the visual. It is the difference between an audit and a hit piece.

- It does not prove he never made the call somewhere non-public or since deleted.
- It does not prove any individual market view of his is wrong.
- It does not prove intent to defraud.

**What it does prove:** the specific public, checkable claim — *"I was the only one publicly calling the exact bottom at $16,000 and the top at $126,000"* — is not supported by his public record, and is contradicted by it.
