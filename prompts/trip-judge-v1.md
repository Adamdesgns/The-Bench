## Steps

2. `node scripts/regime.mjs` (read the day's stamp) and `node scripts/rs.mjs --ticker <SYM>`.
3. Pull live through the Robinhood MCP: `get_equity_quotes` for <SYM>, SPY, QQQ; `get_equity_historicals` 5-minute bars for today's session and daily bars for the last 90 days; `get_portfolio` for the margin account (number from `get_accounts`, the one ending 6137; the 10% band is its total_value x 0.10, recomputed now, never re-asked); `get_earnings_calendar` or `get_earnings_results` for the next print.
4. Judge it on v36, as the source row's plan described it, delta-style. The checks that decide most trips:
   - **Timing.** Before 09:00 CT the first 30-minute bar has not closed: the answer is WAIT with the exact hold test ("BUY only if the 09:00 CT bar closes inside X-Y"). After 14:00 CT on a Friday: NO, Monday re-look (no Friday-close entries). A macro print inside the next hour is named.
   - **The plan's own band.** Price outside the plan's zone or over its ceiling is NO, do not chase. The ceiling is never raised. (On a pump-watch call the plan is the opening-range break, see PUMP WATCH.)
   - **2:1 to a verified pivot** with the stop at least 1.0 ATR(14) under entry, under real structure (tightest legal stop). Report the moving-average R:R too when it differs (P-059).
   - **Capital Competition, both rs windows.** Trails SPY on both, or QQQ on both = No Trade. A split is reported, not a cap.
   - **Earnings inside the hold** = NO. Bull mode, the dated-catalyst lane and the short gates apply as v36 writes them. A short goes on the margin account ••6137; options are level 2 only (no spreads).
5. **Log it.** BUY: `node scripts/log-call.mjs --type long --invalid <stop> ...` with `--call` starting "TRIP JUDGE" and carrying the ticket, grades, `--readiness`, `--origin routine`; then add it to the paper book at the exact ticket size, lane `trip-judge` (`node scripts/paper-book.mjs add ... --lane trip-judge --exit-by <date>`). NO or WAIT: `--type pass` (or `conditional` with `--trigger` and `--decide-by` when there is a live re-look level). Every level you name gets a watcher: `node scripts/mind-changer.mjs add --row B-### ...`. Note the row id.
6. **Text Adam.** Write `docs/reads/<YYYY-MM-DD>-trip-<SYM>.md`. Its FIRST line is the whole answer, plain English, one of:

   - `WAIT <SYM>: <the exact test and the time it resolves>. [B-###]`
   - `NO <SYM>: <the one reason that decided it>. Re-look: <the level or date>. [B-###]`
   Then at most five short lines: the level that tripped, the live price and time, the regime, the R:R, the rs split. Send it: `node scripts/ntfy-read.mjs --slot trip --file docs/reads/<file> --title "<BUY|WAIT|NO> <SYM> - trip judge"`.
7. Commit what you wrote, the same day (the book rule): `git add db/archive.json db/mind-changers.json db/paper-book.json docs/reads/<file>` then `git commit -m "trip judge <SYM>: <BUY|WAIT|NO> B-###"` with the Co-Authored-By line from the session. ## PUMP WATCH (added 2026-11-04, Adam: "If I say watch a ticker like amazon for the pump. You run the hunters and set a 5 min check until lunch time and find out which direction it's likely heading.")
When the prompt says THIS IS A PUMP-WATCH CALL, the trigger is not a book level. The name is on pump watch (`db/pump-watch.json`; Adam or the 5:40a catalyst search put it there) and the 5-minute read (`server/pumpWatch.js`) just turned UP (over the first-30-minute high and VWAP on 1.5x+ volume pace) or DOWN (the mirror). Run steps 1-7 with these changes:
- **THE PUMP-WATCH EXCEPTION (v36; Adam, 2026-11-04: "Yes, override it").** The prompt's `v36 PUMP-WATCH EXCEPTION:` line, computed from who added the watch, decides whether it can apply:
  - **NOT ELIGIBLE** (the desk added the watch itself, or a routine added it after 09:00 CT): step 1 runs as written. Any decision dated today on this ticker stands: `NO <SYM>: entry check final today (v33, B-###)`.
  - **ELIGIBLE** (Adam added it, or the 5:40a catalyst search added it before 09:00 CT): look at each row dated today on this ticker and decide whether it is the SAME plan. It is the same plan when it is in the same direction (long vs UP, short vs DOWN) AND its zone, ceiling or trigger sits within 1.0 ATR(14) of the pump entry (the opening-range high for UP, the low for DOWN).
    - **Same plan:** its answer stands, `NO <SYM>: already judged at 9:02 in B-###`. Never re-plan it (same zone, raised ceiling, wider band, moved stop).
    - **Different plan** (for example AMZN 11/04: a 226-231 pullback zone vs a 270.76 opening-range high): it does not decide this call. Judge the call live as below, and put one line on the card: `9:02 NO on B-### was a different plan (zone X-Y); judged live`.
  - **Step 4's "plan's own band" check reads the pump entry, not the older row's zone.** The band here is the opening-range break with the ceiling below. An old pullback zone being far below price is not a reason for NO.
- **When the answer is NO or WAIT, the card still does the job he asked for:** say the direction the tape is heading and why, and write the next-session plan (the hold test for tomorrow's first 30-minute bar, the stop, the target, the size) as a `conditional` row with `--trigger` and `--decide-by`.
