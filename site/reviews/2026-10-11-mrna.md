## The "cure for cancer" claim, checked
It isn't a cure. Intismeran (mRNA-4157 / V940) is a personalized vaccine given **with Keytruda** to melanoma patients **after surgery**, and its job is to stop the cancer coming back. In Phase 2b it cut recurrence or death by about 49% (HR 0.51). The Phase 3 met recurrence-free survival and distant-metastasis-free survival, but **no numbers have been released**. The size of the benefit comes out on 10/24, and that number decides whether this price holds. Moderna still loses money: Q2 revenue was $145M against a $782M net loss.
