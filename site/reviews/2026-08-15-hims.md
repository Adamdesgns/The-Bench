**🔴 THIS IS A P-008 COUNTER-INSTANCE and it is logged as one.** The pattern sits at 3-for-3 `supported`; this is its first failure and it goes in the book as `holds: false`. Recording it matters more than protecting the pattern — that is the whole point of the falsification test. **The refinement it suggests:** a raise outranks a miss when the miss is *timing*; it does **not** outrank a miss when the miss is *structural margin*, because then the guide is the thing being discounted, not the thing being priced.

## THE WHY

Hims & Hers just told the market it will grow revenue nearly 50% next quarter and raised its full-year outlook, and the stock has fallen four days in a row. That is not the market being slow. Revenue is growing because the company shifted from cheap compounded weight-loss drugs to branded ones, and branded drugs carry far less margin — gross margin dropped from 76% to 64% in a year, and a business that earned $42 million in the same quarter last year lost $86 million this time. So the growth is real and the profit went the other way. The chart agrees: it lost its last major trend line two days ago and closed at the low of the week. There is a genuine bull case here — a director bought over a million dollars of stock in May, no insider sold into the bad quarter, and if margins have found a floor near 64% the growth rate makes this cheap. But none of that is confirmed yet, and the way to find out costs nothing: wait for it to take back the 200-day line, or wait for a drop to the July lows where the buying already happened.
