**Not close:** IDXX (+10.1)

Directionally honest, loosely worded. But the framing hides the split that decides everything:

- **Basing on the MA** (constructive): HD, TMUS, DIS, SPGI, MDLZ, KR, TROW, SYK, WYNN, SMCI, OXY, KDP
- **Falling through the MA** (knives): ISRG (−13.9% last 4 wkly closes), ORCL, QCOM, NXPI, CARR, NCLH, HAL — tagging the MA while falling is not the same as holding it
- **"At the MA" because the stock went nowhere** (artifact): QSR — 6.6% off its high; the MA caught up to a flat stock. APD is 2.9% off its 52-week high — there is no discount there at all.

A 200-week MA touch is a *location*, not a *setup*.

## The verdict

**"Do we need to acquire any?" — No. Not one of the 40 is a buy at Thursday's open.**

Two names earned triggers (OXY 59.60, DIS 105.25). Four were passed with re-arm conditions. And the capital competition is not academic: the free capital already has a #1 claim — the GDS buy zone at the open (B-087), a guide-driven P-008 reaction with 3:1–5.4:1 R:R at the right entry. Nothing on this list beats that trade today.

The list's own slogan is the trap. "Buy great companies at a discount" assumes the 200-week MA *is* the discount. Seventeen of forty were actually there; of those, a third were falling knives passing through the level, and the six best all failed the same question: *what confirms that the discount is being bought?* Until a trigger fires, a discount is just a lower price on the way to a lower price.
