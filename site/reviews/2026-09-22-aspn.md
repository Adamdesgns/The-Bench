## What it is
Aerogel insulation. Two businesses: **Thermal Barrier**, sheets that stop EV battery fires from spreading (GM, now Jaguar Land Rover and other European OEMs), and **Energy Industrial**, insulation for LNG plants, pipelines and refineries. $455M market cap, 83M shares, $153M cash, $57M debt.

2025 broke the business: US EV incentives changed, GM volumes dropped, and in April 2026 the East Providence plant had an incident that forced a staged restart. Revenue halved and margins collapsed. **The whole thesis is the Q3 guide**, a turn back to positive EBITDA. The 9/15 investor deck **reaffirmed** that guide (it did not raise it) and cut the breakeven revenue target 47%, to ~$175M a year by 2027. Europe is ramping ($20–30M in 2026, targeting $40–60M in 2027, JLR award). There is also an $8.9M insurance receivable due this quarter.

**The Guide Rule on this name (P-008):** the Q2 print was bad (EPS −0.22 vs −0.21 est) and the guide was strong, and on 8/6 the stock followed the guide: 5.01 → 6.89 close (+37.5%, 14.4M shares vs ~1M normal), trading as high as 8.10. **Then it gave back all of it.** It bled for six weeks to 4.76 on 9/10, *below* the pre-print close. The market heard the guide and didn't believe it would last. Logged as a P-008 persistence failure. The reason is probably execution: a 7% gross margin company promising a 30–60% revenue jump the quarter after a plant incident.
